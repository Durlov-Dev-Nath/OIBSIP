## Overview
This folder contains the official logo design for a fictional real estate and housing brand completed as part of the **Oasis Infobyte Graphic Design Internship**.

## Brand Details
* **Brand Name:** Dream House
* **Slogan:** Where Dreams Find Their Address
* **Industry:** Real Estate / Housing / Construction
* **Design Philosophy:** Minimalist and modern concept blending the letters 'D' and 'H' with architectural structures and nature elements to display growth, trust, and shelter.

## Logo Variants Included
* **Logo_Full_Colour.png:** The primary brand identity showcasing the vibrant green, blue, and orange theme.
* **Logo_Monochrome.png:** Solid black version for single-color printing and light backgrounds.
* **Logo_Reversed.png:** Solid white version optimized for dark backdrops.
