## Overview

This repository contains my Task 01 projects completed as part of the **OBISIP Graphic Design Internship**.

The task focuses on creating visually appealing and effective **posters and flyers** for promotional and awareness purposes.

## Project 01 – Meena Bazar Promotional Flyer

A promotional flyer designed for a grocery store campaign featuring fresh products, special offers, product pricing, and free delivery.

### Key Design Elements

* Promotional sale section
* Product showcase with pricing
* Fresh and vibrant visual presentation
* Call-to-action elements
* Free delivery promotion
* Clear product information and hierarchy

## Project 02 – Child Therapy Social Media Poster

A social media awareness poster designed for a child therapy and speech development center.

### Key Design Elements

* Bengali headline and supporting message
* Child-focused imagery
* Clean and professional layout
* Blue and white color scheme
* Clear contact information
* Emotional and informative visual communication
